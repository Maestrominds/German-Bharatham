Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the German Bharatham mobile application (herein referred to as the "Application") and website, created and operated to connect the community, manage listings, housing/accommodation, jobs, and community services.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number) when you register an account.
  • Information you provide when creating listings, jobs, accommodations, or community service posts.
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address).
  • Device name, operating system version, and system configuration.

Data Retention Policy & Account Deletion

We will retain user-provided data for as long as you use the Application. If you want to delete your account or any associated data, you can do so directly within the settings page in the Application or request deletion online.

Please visit our Data Deletion Request Page to submit an account deletion request. Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete all your user profile details, listings, and associated data from our servers within 30 days.

Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. We provide physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information we process and maintain.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
